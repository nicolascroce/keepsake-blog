Capture, organize, publish: Keepsake helps you turn an idea into a published post, on your public page. But a post doesn't live in a vacuum — it deserves to travel. This update extends that path, from your note all the way to your readers, wherever they are.

<mark>Connect Typefully to broadcast your published notes to your networks, share links as a rich preview card, and copy a note's content in whichever format you need.</mark>

## Publish to your socials, from Keepsake

You write in Keepsake. You publish on your page. And then? Too often, you have to copy everything elsewhere to share it on X or Threads. That detour is gone.

<mark>Connect your Typefully account, and every published note can go out to your networks with a single button.</mark>

You connect once, in Settings: add your Typefully API key, pick your social set and the networks you want to post to — X, Threads, and more. From then on, every published post carries a share action. One click, and it joins your Typefully queue, ready to go out on your schedule. Need to send it again? You can, anytime.

Typefully stays your control tower: scheduling, threads, previews. Keepsake simply hands off to it — your writing workshop and your distribution tool, finally connected.

## Share a link, beautifully

Not every post is text. Sometimes what you want to share is a find: an article, a tool, a page worth the detour.

<mark>A new post type arrives: the link.</mark> Paste a URL, and your public page won't just show a bare address. It composes a polished card — the site's image, title and description — just like the best platforms. Your readers see at a glance where you're taking them, and why it's worth the click.

It's a simple way to make your public page a place for curation, not just writing.

## Copy your content, in the right format

Your words are yours, and they should leave Keepsake as easily as they entered.

<mark>A new "Copy content" menu appears on your notes</mark>, from the card and from the editor alike. Grab the text in one click, in the format that suits you: plain text to paste anywhere, Markdown to keep your formatting. Ready to move to an email, a document, another tool.

## Over to you

Everything is live right now. Connect Typefully in your settings, and watch your next published note reach your networks with a button. Paste a link into a note, and see the card compose itself.

For the details, the help center has you covered: [Connect Typefully to publish to your social networks](https://keepsake.place/help/connect-typefully).
